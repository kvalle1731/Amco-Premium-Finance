06 / INSIGHT

A little more
clarity.

What is insurance premium financing?+

It is a financial arrangement that may allow an eligible insurance premium to be paid through an initial payment and scheduled installments under an agreement. Interest, fees and other terms may apply.

Does AMCO provide the insurance coverage?+

No. Insurance agencies and carriers handle coverage, eligibility and underwriting. AMCO's focus is insurance premium financing.

Can I get a binding rate quote online?+

Not at this stage. The financing illustration is educational only, and the inquiry form prepares an email. A representative must review potential financing terms.

What should I have ready to begin?+

The policy type, location, effective date and total premium are useful starting points. Further documents may be requested separately through approved channels.
