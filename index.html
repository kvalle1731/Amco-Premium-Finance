GOOD TO KNOW

Answers that move
things forward.

Premium finance should feel understandable, even when insurance isn't simple. Here are the fundamentals.

What is insurance premium financing?+

Premium financing is an arrangement that can allow an eligible policyholder to make an upfront payment and finance the remaining insurance premium, typically with finance charges and scheduled installments. Exact terms depend on the written agreement and applicable rules.

Can AMCO help with commercial, auto and homeowners insurance?+

Those are the insurance categories AMCO intends to serve. Availability depends on the state, policy, underwriting criteria and financing program. Contact us to discuss the type of coverage involved.

Does premium financing replace the insurance carrier?+

No. The insurance policy remains with the insurer. Premium financing relates to how the eligible insurance premium is paid, not who provides the insurance coverage.

How are payments and finance charges determined?+

The approved finance agreement specifies the down payment, the amount financed, applicable charges, due dates and other terms. The planning tool on this website does not calculate finance charges or provide binding terms.

Can I apply or make payments online today?+

Not through this website at this stage. The current website provides information and a way to contact AMCO. No financing applications, payments or underwriting decisions are processed here.
