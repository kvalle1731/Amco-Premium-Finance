What is insurance premium financing?

Premium financing is an arrangement in which eligible insurance premium costs are financed and repaid over time under a finance agreement. A down payment, financing charges, and scheduled installments may apply.

Does AMCO sell insurance policies?

No. The purpose of this website is to discuss financing the cost of insurance premiums, not to provide insurance underwriting or policy quotes.

Can I get an instant finance quote here?

Not currently. You can prepare an email inquiry with the basic policy information. AMCO will need to review eligible requests and approved terms separately.

Is the website a customer payment portal?

No. Online payments, account servicing, and digital finance agreements are not enabled on this staging website.

What information do I need to start?

Typically the policy type, state, premium amount, effective date, and your contact details. Additional information may be required to assess an actual financing arrangement.
